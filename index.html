The four important areas we explore

The same four we would cover in the six-month Lead with Clarity partnership — applied to one issue instead of many. Your situation decides where we spend the time.

1

What is actually happening?

We separate the situation from the noise around it and examine the narratives you have built on top of it. Leaders rarely get stuck for lack of commitment. They get stuck because an unexamined assumption has quietly narrowed what they can see.

2

What is yours to carry?

Some of this is genuinely yours to decide and own. Some belongs to someone else, to a conversation you have been putting off, to a boundary you have not drawn, or to a truth you have been reluctant to face. We name the difference.

3

What do your strengths, values and convictions make possible here?

God has shaped you with particular strengths, values and convictions. The wisest path forward usually works with that design rather than against it.

4

What is the next faithful step?

We look at the paths open to you and what follows from each: what happens if you act, if you wait, and if you do nothing. Then we work toward the step that actually needs to happen next.

Questions leaders ask before booking

Will you tell me what decision to make?+
No. My role is not to make the decision for you, but to help you think more clearly, examine the situation more fully, and discern what needs your attention next. Leaders who are handed an answer rarely own it. Leaders who reach their own answer act on it.
What kind of issue should I bring?+
One real leadership issue that feels important, unresolved, or difficult to process alone: a personnel decision, a difficult conversation, a team issue, a restructuring question, or a strategic decision.
Can I bring more than one issue?+
The intensive focuses on one primary issue. One issue explored properly produces more clarity than two issues touched briefly.
Is this confidential?+
Yes. The conversation is private and confidential within the normal ethical boundaries of professional coaching. Nothing you bring is shared, referenced or used elsewhere.
How is this different from the free Clarity Conversation?+
The Clarity Conversation is a 30-minute exploratory conversation about whether ongoing coaching is a good fit. The Decision Clarity Intensive is a full coaching session on one specific issue you are carrying right now. You come with an issue and leave with greater clarity about it.
How do we schedule, and can I reschedule?+
You choose your slot and complete payment together on my booking page. Once booked you receive a confirmation email with the Zoom link and reminders. Please also add it to your own calendar so it is protected in your week. If something urgent comes up you can reschedule up to 48 hours before the session.
What if I need more than one session?+
Some issues resolve in one focused conversation. Others are the visible edge of something larger, and one session brings that into view rather than settling it. If that is what we find, I will say so. Rather than booking a series of separate intensives, the better path is usually ongoing coaching — most senior leaders are not carrying one issue but several, alongside the pressures that keep arriving.
