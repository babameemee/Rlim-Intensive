Three things before we meet

Put it in your own calendar

Add the session to your calendar as soon as you can, and protect the half hour on either side of it if your week allows. The conversation goes further when you are not arriving from one meeting and leaving for another.

Tell me what you are bringing

Reply to your confirmation email with a few sentences about the leadership issue or decision you want to work on. A paragraph is plenty. It means no part of our ninety minutes is spent on background.

Come as you are on the day

No preparation, no documents, no tidy summary required. Bring the issue in the state it is actually in. If something urgent comes up, you can reschedule up to 48 hours before the session.

What the session will look like

Ninety unhurried minutes on one issue, moving through the four areas we explore: what is actually happening, what is yours to carry, what your strengths, values and convictions make possible, and what the next faithful step is. Afterwards you receive your written Decision Clarity Summary — a record of the themes and considerations to keep thinking with.

One last thing

You have already done the hardest part, which was deciding not to carry this alone for another month. I am looking forward to our conversation.
